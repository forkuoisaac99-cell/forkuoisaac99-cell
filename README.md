# BIOGRAPHY OF AM FORKUO ISAAC

My name is **Am Forkuo Isaac**, and I hail from Kumasi in the Ashanti Region of Ghana. I was born and raised in Kumasi on 30th March 1995. I am an Akan by tribe and currently single.

## Educational Background

I began my educational journey at Techiman Good Shepherd Educational Institute, where I completed my Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE). I subsequently continued my education at Kumasi High School, where I obtained my West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE). I later pursued and obtained my first degree from the University of Education, Winneba.

## Family Background

I am the third-born child of my parents. My father is Mr. Peter Awisi, and my mother is Miss Comfort Takyiewaa. I value my family greatly and appreciate the support and guidance they have provided throughout my life.

## Military Career

I joined the Ghana Armed Forces in 2020. After completing my basic infantry training, I was posted to the 41 Signal Defence Regiment. I was later transferred to the 174 Electronic Warfare Cyber Signal Regiment, where I continued my military career.

Throughout my military journey, I have had the opportunity to undertake several professional military courses that have enhanced my knowledge, skills, and competence in my field of work.

Some of the military courses I have completed include:

- Signal Basic Course
- Electronic Warfare Course
- Drone Piloting and Control System Course

I am a proud soldier who is dedicated to serving my country and protecting its citizens. I believe in discipline, respect, honesty, and teamwork. My profession has taught me the importance of responsibility, cooperation, and commitment. As a soldier, I strive to remain focused, disciplined, and devoted to my duties, even in challenging situations.

## Personal Interests and Ambitions

Outside my professional duties, I enjoy spending quality time with my family and friends. I also enjoy learning new things, maintaining physical fitness, and developing my personal skills.

I believe in treating everyone with kindness and respect, regardless of their background. My ambition is to continue improving myself both professionally and personally while making a positive impact on society.

I remain committed to serving my country with honour, dedication, and professionalism while striving for excellence in all aspects of my life.
